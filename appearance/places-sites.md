# Places sites: aggregator carousel and refinement chip

> Mirrored from the official Google Search Central documentation at [https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/places-sites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/places-sites). Page content, including any embedded figures, is authored by Google and was last updated by Google on 2026-09-11.[^google-places-sites]

This page describes features for Places sites in Google Search, including the aggregator carousel and refinement chips for local business and hotel queries in Türkiye. Publishers don't need to add any markup to be eligible for the aggregator carousel or refinement chips.

![Places sites aggregator carousel and refinement chip in Google Search](https://developers.google.com/static/search/docs/images/places-sites.png)

The "Places sites" aggregator carousel allows users to easily see the top aggregator results for their respective query. Users can also click **More sites** to see additional relevant aggregator sites for their query.

The "Places sites" refinement chip can help users focus the Search page on aggregator text results. Searchers can activate the refinement chips at the top of the Search results page or by clicking **More sites** on the aggregator carousel.

## Feature availability

These new experiences are available to users in Türkiye who are searching for queries related to local businesses and hotels.

If your business serves users in Türkiye and you would like to learn more and express interest in these new experiences, you can start by filling out the [interest form](https://support.google.com/websearch/contact/search_aggregator_features).
