# Top Places List

> Mirrored from the official Google Search Central documentation at [https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/top-places-list](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/top-places-list). Page content, including any embedded figures, is authored by Google and was last updated by Google on 2025-12-10.[^google-top-places-list]

If your business is mentioned in any "top places" lists online, Google might show a rich result that shows the lists that mention your business. For example, if your restaurant appears in a "Top 10 Chinese restaurants in NYC" list and a "Best restaurants in the city" list on [qualified websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/top-places-list/#requirements), these lists would be shown in your business's search result. This feature is only for businesses with a physical location.

## Requirements for the listing site

In order for your site's "top places" list to be eligible to appear in a Top Places List result, the list must meet these criteria:

- The list must be curated by the content provider, be genuine, independent, and not sponsored.
- The list must not consist of templated sentences built from data or automated metrics.
- The list must not contain vulgar or potentially offensive language.

## Prevent your website's lists from appearing in a Top Places List feature

To prevent lists from your site from appearing in a Top Places List, [opt out of display in local search results and other Google properties](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/3035947).
